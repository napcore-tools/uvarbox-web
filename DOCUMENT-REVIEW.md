# Historical technical documentation — editorial review register

**Discussion draft · 20 November 2026 · Not a public-download approval**

This register supports a discussion with the partners reviewing the recovered UVAR Box website. The documents were inventoried in the supplied `uvarbox-content-v2.zip`; **that source archive and its historical PDFs are not bundled with this website repository**. Paths below are relative to the *source archive*, not to `docs/public/`.

Only the three previously hosted editions appear in `docs/public/publications/`: the official EU Final Report, the 2022 brochure and the 2021 project leaflet. The latter two still require confirmation of redistribution rights. This register does not add or approve any further downloads. The entries below are **candidate historical publications** and do not imply that their contents or data remain current.

## Decision options for each candidate

- **Host:** include a reviewed PDF in the public website, subject to written republication permission, attribution and content checks.
- **Link:** point to a verified, lawful public publisher or archival edition instead of copying it.
- **Cite:** refer to the publication bibliographically, without a PDF download.
- **Exclude:** do not promote it from this site.

All entries currently have **decision pending**. Record the decision, approver and source URL in the column or in a related GitHub issue before publishing any file.

## Checks before approving a publication

1. Verify that the PDF opens, the version is complete and the stated page count is correct.
2. Verify the public provenance, final publication status, copyright, publisher and redistribution permission. A recovered website link is **not** itself a licence.
3. Review names, email addresses, photographs, map tiles and any third-party materials for privacy and reuse rights.
4. Check that dated ambitions, trial software and sample traffic regulations are not presented as currently available or authoritative.
5. Choose **Host**, **Link**, **Cite** or **Exclude** and record the responsible reviewer.
6. If **Host** is approved, add only the cleared PDF to `docs/public/publications/` and its exact filename to `permittedDocuments` in `scripts/check-content.mjs`; then verify its link and rebuild the site.

The original UVAR Box application, legacy project source code, unpublished draft poster, internal assessments, personal correspondence and sample regulation XML are **not** candidates for this public document list.
