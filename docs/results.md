# The 2022 pilot results

<span class="page-kicker">EU preparatory action · 2022 results, official EU report edition 2025</span>

The UVAR Box Final Report records **570 digitised urban vehicle access regulation entries** across six countries. Its five focus Member States were Austria, Belgium, Germany, Italy and the Netherlands, with additional best-effort work in Portugal.

The single Austrian pedestrian-zone entry represented 260 streets in the project report. A record is therefore not necessarily equivalent to an individual street, regulatory instrument or present-day UVAR scheme.

## What the pilot demonstrated

- The team developed a DATEX II UVAR structure and a web-based tool to generate, import, edit and export records.
- Country coaches supported digitisation in five focus Member States, with additional work elsewhere.
- Germany and the Netherlands used national arrangements to publish digitised LEZ data via their respective national access points during the pilot.
- The work demonstrated different routes for providing digital information and highlighted the need for maintenance, validation and cooperation with competent authorities.

## What the numbers do not establish

The 570 entries were an **end-of-project snapshot from 2022**. They do not mean that 570 records are currently downloadable, maintained, verified by the issuing cities or suitable for operational journey guidance.

The official Final Report explains that in several countries, country coaches digitised rules on behalf of cities and that city-level validation was not completed for every record.

## How to use this evidence

Use these figures to understand what the EU pilot produced in 2022. For live driving restrictions, consult the issuing city or other responsible authority. Before any dataset is republished for operational reuse, its coverage, provenance, licence, quality and refresh date should be established.
