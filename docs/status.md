# Status and next steps

<span class="page-kicker">Provisional public information · 19 November 2026</span>

UVAR Box began as a European preparatory action in 2020–2022. The original team developed a tool and data model to help authorities describe urban vehicle access regulations (UVARs) in a machine-readable format. This website preserves the documented results and opens a place for discussion about future needs.

<div class="page-callout"><p><strong>What is available now:</strong> this informational website, explanations of the historical pilot and a selection of its public project publications.</p></div>

## Direction under discussion for 2027

The working material identifies four broad areas to explore:

1. **Reassess the historic assets.** Evaluate the 2022 data model, application and available records before deciding what remains useful.
2. **Prepare a responsible public release.** Establish ownership, licences, third-party dependencies, privacy and security requirements before publishing any legacy code or data.
3. **Modernise where justified.** Consider security, usability, maintenance and compatibility with the relevant DATEX II specifications and current data-exchange practices.
4. **Work with the ecosystem.** Discuss concrete workflows with cities, National Access Points (NAPs), service providers and data consumers.

This is a **discussion agenda**, not a delivery commitment, funded roadmap or announcement that a modernised platform exists. The original application and its data would need independent technical and publication review before any release.

## How stakeholders can help

The next useful questions concern who maintains each regulation, how conditions and exemptions are represented, which checks demonstrate fitness for reuse, how changes propagate to NAPs, and what service providers need to consume the information accurately.

See [Get involved](/contribute) for a structured set of questions. Public, source-backed corrections to these pages may be raised through the [repository's issue form](https://github.com/napcore-tools/uvarbox-web/issues/new?template=content-update.yml).

## Publication principles

- **Authority first.** The competent authority remains the source for binding and current access rules. The historic UVAR records cannot replace it.
- **Dates and versions matter.** Do not label a 2022 record as current or a draft plan as implemented.
- **Validate meaning, not only syntax.** DATEX II/XML conformance by itself is insufficient to establish completeness, legal accuracy or freshness.
- **Release deliberately.** Public software, data, screenshots, contacts and documents require appropriate review and distribution rights.
