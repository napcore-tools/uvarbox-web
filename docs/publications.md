# Public project publications

<span class="page-kicker">Historical references · 2021–2025 editions</span>

These publications document the UVAR Box project and its outputs from 2020–2022. The three editions below were checked against their public publication records and the corresponding PDF files. They are provided for historical and background reading, **not as a source of current vehicle access restrictions**.

## How to read the historic figures

The figure of **570** digitised UVAR entries used elsewhere on this site is drawn from Table 2 of the official Final Report. It describes the 2022 project results and does not establish the current validity or availability of individual regulations.

The interface shown on the [workflow page](/tool) is a historical view of the original UVAR Box tool, with map attribution retained.
