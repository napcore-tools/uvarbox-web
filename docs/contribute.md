# Help shape reliable UVAR information

<span class="page-kicker">Stakeholder questions · provisional discussion</span>

For urban vehicle access regulations to be understandable in journey-planning and mobility services, the information must be both machine-readable and dependable. UVAR Box explored the digitisation step in 2022. These questions identify what a future iteration would need to solve.

No public technical support, city onboarding programme or production tool is being offered on this page. The questions below are invitations to inform future scoping.

## Cities and authorities

What would make it practical to digitise and maintain the regulations you issue?

- Which zones need to be described: LEZs, limited traffic zones, pedestrian areas, parking rules or other restrictions?
- How are geometry, temporal rules, exemptions and vehicle categories created and updated today?
- Who can authorise a record, confirm its correctness and report a change or withdrawal?
- Would mapping tools, structured forms, templates, import from existing systems or another route best fit your workflow?

## National Access Points

How should trusted UVAR information reach the NAP and remain discoverable?

- Which DATEX II versions or other supported formats and metadata are relevant to your publication process?
- How can provenance, validity periods, licence terms, competent authority and update frequency be recorded?
- What technical and semantic checks should be applied before publishing or refreshing data?
- What would help automate submissions and corrections without losing accountability?

## Service providers and data users

What do you require to give travellers correct, understandable access advice?

- Which vehicle attributes, permits, exceptions, languages and time-dependent details are essential?
- How should a service distinguish verified, incomplete, superseded and missing records?
- What update frequency and change notifications are needed to avoid stale guidance?
- Which test cases, examples, APIs or documentation would make integration easier?

## Suggest a correction or share a public requirement

Once the repository is live and GitHub Issues are enabled, open a [content update issue](https://github.com/napcore-tools/uvarbox-web/issues/new?template=content-update.yml). Describe:

1. The page or workflow you are addressing.
2. The observed problem, requirement or proposed correction.
3. A public source and date, when making a factual claim.
4. Whether the item relates to a **2022 pilot result**, a **currently verified practice**, or a **future proposal**.

You can also [inspect the website source](https://github.com/napcore-tools/uvarbox-web). This is a provisional public discussion channel, **not** a mechanism to submit confidential data, credentials, personal information, sensitive system details or unapproved regulatory records. Use the normal organisational coordination channel for those matters.

For context, review [how the original tool worked](/tool), the [2022 pilot results](/results) and [what remains proposed](/status).
